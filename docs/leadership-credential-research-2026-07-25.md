## Read this first — two issues that outrank the accolades

### 1. Frank Fernandez does not appear on the company's own team page

`https://www.endocyclictherapeutics.com/team` lists **six** people: Tanya
Petrossian, Melanie Hartsough, David Lin, Andrea Lukes, Aileen Ryan, and
Miganush Stepanians. A targeted search of that page's full text for
"Fernandez", "Frank", "CFO", "Chief Financial", "finance", "advisor",
"consultant", and "board" returned **no matches**.

A general web search does assert he is CFO, but the top result backing that
assertion is the same team page that does not contain his name, so I treat the
assertion as unconfirmed rather than corroborating.

**This matters most because we present him as Chief Financial Officer** — the
strongest employment claim on the page — on the thinnest evidence of the six.
His bio is also the only one with no specific, checkable credential
("decades of financial leadership experience"). Please confirm he is current
and should be listed, or remove the entry.

### 2. The "Officers & functional leads" framing may overstate the relationship

Research indicates **five of the six hold principal or senior roles at their own
or other firms**:

| Person | Principal affiliation found |
| --- | --- |
| Dr. Miganush Stepanians | Founder, President & CEO, PROMETRIKA (CRO, founded 2003) |
| Aileen Ryan | Senior Regulatory Advisor, PROMETRIKA |
| Dr. Melanie Hartsough | Owner, Hartsough Nonclinical Consulting |
| Dr. David Lin | TS Pharma Experts LLC; Senior Consultant, Biologics Consulting Group |
| Dr. Andrea Lukes | Founder, Carolina Women's Research & Wellness Center |

Two of them (Stepanians and Ryan) are at the **same CRO**, which reads like an
engaged vendor relationship rather than in-house officers.

Notably, **EndoCyclic's own site groups all six under a neutral "Our Team"
heading with no officer, advisor, or consultant distinction.** Our page makes a
stronger claim than the company's own public site does. For an investor
audience this is a material distinction, and it is the kind of thing diligence
checks. Consider whether "Development team" (the framing this site used before
July 24) or an explicit advisor/consultant label is more accurate.

## Recommended actions

1. **Confirm or remove Frank Fernandez**, and reconsider the "Chief Financial
   Officer" title if he is retained.
2. **Correct Andrea Lukes' trial count** from "more than 90" to a figure you can
   evidence — public sources support "over 75". Confirm the "over 30 years"
   figure, and consider adding **MHSc** to her name.
3. **Confirm David Lin's "over 27 years"** against his current CV.
4. **Confirm Aileen Ryan's "MS in Basic Medical Sciences."**
5. **Decide the framing** — "Officers & functional leads" vs the company's own
   neutral "Our Team", given five of six hold principal roles elsewhere.
6. **Consider adding the strongest omitted credentials**, especially
   Stepanians' PROMETRIKA founder/CEO role and Ryan's specific Ludwig and Bayer
   titles. Both make the bench look stronger, not weaker.

Once you decide, I can apply the changes. I have not edited any bio.
