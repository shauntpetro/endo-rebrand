Company overview

EndoCyclic Therapeutics, Inc. is a clinical-stage precision medicine company in Irvine, California. Founder and CEO Dr. Tanya Petrossian, PhD leads the company as it advances non-hormonal precision peptide programs for endometriosis and oncology.

The platform combines pH-mediated activation with selective uptake by diseased tissue through a proprietary endocytic pathway. It is designed to act in diseased tissue while avoiding hormones, surgery, and systemic toxicity.

Endometriosis context

Scale and delay

  • 10% of reproductive-age women globally
  • 8-year average diagnostic delay
  • Leading cause of infertility and chronic pelvic pain

Market and care gap

  • $180B-$250B global treatment-market potential, based on a McKinsey estimate
  • Current therapies are largely hormone-based and symptomatic
  • Current therapies do not eliminate lesions or modify disease biology
Lead program: ENDO-205 is a first-in-class, non-hormonal precision peptide therapeutic with FDA IND Allowance (2026), now in Phase 1.
