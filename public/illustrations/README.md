Prompt and factual guardrails are documented in `docs/illustration-specs.md`.
Every generated plate must remain conceptual, carry an on-page disclosure, and
avoid simulated clinical scans, performance data, measurements, or treatment
outcomes unless those claims are explicitly approved in `truth.md`.

Every peptide-bearing plate uses the same conceptual 13-bead platform motif
documented by `docs/assets/canonical-endo-peptide-v1.svg`: clockwise from the
orange bead at 12 o’clock, one orange, five warm-taupe, three violet, three
orange, and one warm-taupe bead (four orange, six warm-taupe, and three violet
in total). Targeting, selective uptake, pH-mediated activation, and program
context are shown around the intact peptide—not by recoloring or changing its
bead count. Any intermediate organelle or teal contact-pocket detail is an
abstract visual device derived from the user-supplied reference, not an
additional approved ENDO-205 mechanism or target claim. Public copy remains
limited to `truth.md`.

The ENDO-205 translation and FemLUNA™ targeting masters also serve as source
images for their qualified editorial-use packages in
`public/downloads/media/`.
